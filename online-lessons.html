What lessons are like

Personal, not generic.

Nishant’s drum lessons are heavily equipped with very personal experiences and stories, and act as a therapeutic session for all his students.

He has taught over 120 students one-on-one in Delhi, 70+ in Kathmandu and almost 200 in Guwahati, and teaches over Skype, mainly students from outside North-East India. He learned from Benny Greb in 2011 and has run sold-out workshops in Guwahati, Delhi and Kathmandu.
