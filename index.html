About

About

Nishant Hagjer is a solo drummer from Guwahati, Assam, India, playing especially in the heavy metal scene for the past 20 years. He is a versatile musician, playing a wide range of genres and instruments.

He has toured Europe, the UK, Australia, New Zealand, Vietnam, Nepal and the whole of South East Asia, played Download Festival and Bloodstock, and teaches as a drum teacher and clinician, with sold-out workshops.
